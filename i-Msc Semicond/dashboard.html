    Method notes

    • Ratings use a 1–5 scale; the confidence interval is mean ± 1.96 × SD ÷ √n (normal approximation). With fewer than 30 responses treat intervals as indicative.
    • Verdict: Strong = mean ≥ 4.2 and ≥ 80% favourable; Good = mean ≥ 3.8 and ≥ 65% favourable; Review = mean ≥ 3.3; otherwise Reconsider.
    • Interest groups: “Intent” = would enrol personally or nominate / sponsor staff; “Advocacy” = would encourage family or friends.
    • Fee midpoints used for the median: RM 12,500 · RM 17,500 · RM 22,500; “Others” uses the amount typed by the respondent.
    • All language versions of the survey are combined; the questionnaire answers are always stored in English.
